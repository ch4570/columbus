The task asks for every RepositoryIndex method reaching safe_source through read_stable within three calls under explicitly restricted source-level rules. It counts direct imported calls, lexical self member handoffs inside RepositoryIndex, and read_stable's direct safe_source call. It excludes aliases, callbacks and other routes. This is a source-navigation task, not runtime reachability or a broad impact-completeness claim. The model prompt does not reveal the qualifying method names or their count.

`oracle.py` uses only the standard-library AST and ZIP source, never Columbus or its graph. It verifies the relevant explicit imports and no target-name rebinding, records lexical ownership and call sites, enumerates all RepositoryIndex methods, and computes shortest paths within the declared rule. The reviewed answer is _source, refresh, status (two calls each), and symbol (three). The initial oracle rejected a decorated `_read` method while scanning irrelevant edges; the rule is source-level and its body can be scanned. Receiver parameter/rebinding checks remain. No model trial ran during this correction.
